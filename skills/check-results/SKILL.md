name: check-results
description: Default workflow whenever the user asks how their posts, link clicks, Google Business Profile, or website Google Search SEO traffic are performing, when to post, or what to post more of (even without mentioning Spica Post). Reviews Results, tracked short-link clicks, GBP, Search Console, best times and hashtags across one or all brands.
---

Use this skill when the user asks how their posts performed, how many clicks their links got, how their website performed in Google Search, when to post, or how multiple client brands compare.

1. Call `brands_list`. If the user asks about all brands at once (or manages multiple clients), call `brands_results_summary` and `brands_overview`. For a single brand, pass `brandId` to `growth_get` (days: 7, 30 or 90) and `results_get` (days: 7, 30 or 90; optional `source`: `"all"` default for Spica + synced native posts, `"spica"`, or `"native"`), plus `jobs_query` and `coverage_get` (and `short_links_list` if they ask for per-link click details). If `sync.lastSyncedAt` is null or stale on eligible accounts (or the user asks to refresh their account numbers), call `results_sync` first (15-minute cooldown per account).
2. Summarize per-channel growth stage and health (`growth_get`), topic and content-pillar winners (`byTopic`, `byPillar`), audience follower counts and period growth (`audience` per account and total), engagement rate (`engagementRate`: average per post, % of followers, % of views), content format winners (`byKind`: videos, carousels, photos, text, stories), total posts (`sources` breakdown of Spica vs native), likes, comments, shares, views/reach where reported, tracked short-link clicks (`shortLinks` and per-post clicks), best-performing posts with live links and linked topics, top hashtags and best posting times (`bestTimes`). Never invent external benchmarks or claim X engagement readings (X counts posts and Spica short-link clicks only).
3. If `google` is present in `results_get`, report Google Business Profile calls, direction requests, website clicks and views in Search and Maps with the exact dates covered (`period`). If `searchConsole` is present, report Google Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, top search queries and top landing pages. Use `search_console_status` to check connection status or share `connectLink` (`/results`), `search_console_select` to switch verified website properties, and ask first before calling `search_console_disconnect`. If either says `no_reading` or `reconnect`, explain plainly without blaming the posts.
4. Flag any recently failed deliveries (`jobs_query` with `state: "failed"`) or accounts needing reconnection (`accounts_connect`).
5. Recommend 3 to 5 concrete next topics or posting-time tweaks. Never change posting times (`queue_set`) or schedule posts without the user's yes.
